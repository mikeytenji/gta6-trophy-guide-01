# Leonida Platinum Desk — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Tracker

An offline web app for players chasing the platinum in Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5. It covers all 55 trophies in one place, so you can see the full road to platinum before you start playing and keep track of where you stand as you go.

The heart of the guide is a checklist: every trophy can be ticked off as you earn it, and your progress is saved locally in the browser, so you can close the page and pick up exactly where you left off. A platinum roadmap lays out the sensible order to play through — story, side content, and cleanup — so you know what to aim for first and what can wait until after the credits.

Missable trophies get clear warnings up front, with the point of no return spelled out before you reach it, so a careless story choice never costs you a second playthrough. Each trophy also carries its own citation, pointing to the source behind the requirement, so you can check the details for yourself instead of taking the guide's word for it.
